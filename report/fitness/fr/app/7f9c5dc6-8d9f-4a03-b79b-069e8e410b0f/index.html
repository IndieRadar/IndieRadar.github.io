5 avis récents mentionnent bugs et stabilité.

Fenêtre d'avis : 15 juil. 2026 - 25 sept. 2026

Pourquoi c'est important: Les utilisateurs mentionnent des crashes ou de l'instabilité. À traiter comme actionnable seulement en cas de pic ou après une mise à jour ; sinon souvent du bruit de baseline.

Exemples d'avis:

  1. Gina Lisak, 25 sept. 2026, 1/5: "Since the app changed to Google Health, I have syncing issues and lose data about once per month. I suspect it is because Google keeps trying to force 100% connection to my phone and notifications from several apps. They keep reverting my permissions during updates, without my consent. I had to lock it down at the device level to prevent it. I already have to sync daily, but it is still failing. If a company tries to change my behaviors, I will stop using their products and services."
  2. Renee Carl, 24 sept. 2026, 1/5: "The 9 Sept 2026 update still DOES NOT FIX the stats for swimming. I do not want my pace per mile! No one wants that. New update (June 2026) so many bugs. Absolutely no one tracks their swims in miles or kilometers. What happened to yards and meters? The update also took away average speed per 100. Fix the stupid AI agent providing useless responses to reviews providing specific bugs and problems with the app. Google, you broke fitbit, you need to fix it."
  3. Jim Steenbergen, 16 sept. 2026, 1/5: "Having the same issues as Kimberly on 6/15. Every time I have to update phone the sync gets messed up and I have to leave on charger and start using my "fitbit charge 2" watch, which is much more reliable. Plus it no longer reads my O2 or EKG. only one light on back lights up, where there use to be 3 lights!!!"
