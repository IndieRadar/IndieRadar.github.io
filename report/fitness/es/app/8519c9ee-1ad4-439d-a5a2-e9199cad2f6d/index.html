  1. carabury, 2 sept 2026, 1/5: "Be very careful with the “trial” — there’s an important restriction they don’t make clear. I signed up for a trial through a friend’s referral link and booked a class. I needed to reschedule and tried to do so within the permitted cancellation window. However, the app gave me no option to reschedule and instead told me I needed to sign up for the paid membership. I eventually had to Google the issue to find out why. Apparently, during the trial, you only get one appointment at a given location, and if you cancel that appointment—even within the allowed cancellation period—you lose the ability to book another one during the trial. That is a pretty significant restriction for a “trial,” and I don’t remember seeing it clearly disclosed when I signed up or booked the class. If the purpose of the trial is to let people experience the service, preventing someone from rescheduling a class they canceled within the allowed timeframe is incredibly frustrating. I contacted customer service hoping they could simply restore the trial booking. The chatbot was essentially useless, and when I finally reached a person, I was told they couldn’t help me. I would strongly recommend making this policy much more transparent. Customers shouldn’t have to Google the rules after the fact to discover that a permitted cancellation effectively uses up their entire trial. I’m leaving this review so other people know before they sign up: read the fine print, because “trial” apparently doesn’t mean you can freely reschedule your first class."
