  1. Surgef, 22 août 2026, 1/5: "I had been using Forest for a while but the app is notoriously buggy and can cause a lot of delays/issues. I would recommend not using Forest and instead using Lime as a much more reliable bike app."

Not a single contact channel actually works. On top of that, I regularly lose minutes from my 1,200-minute subscription to broken bikes, and reported bugs never get reimbursed. I had to buy an extra 500-minute bundle, which is now being drained by the exact same issue. The referral bonus I was promised for friends who signed up also never showed up. An app you can't actually reach anyone through when something goes wrong isn't usable. Can't recommend this right now."

I think they just hope you dont notice these charges and over time, give them more money that you dont owe them. Cancelled my subscription to them and switched back to Lime."
