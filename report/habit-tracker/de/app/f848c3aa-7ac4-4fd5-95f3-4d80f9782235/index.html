Werbedruck.

Review-Fenster: 09.07.2026 - 17.08.2026

Warum das zählt: Nutzer spüren Werbedruck. Das signalisiert Monetarisierungsreibung und eine Öffnung für einen saubereren Wettbewerber.

Review-Beispiele:

  1. TDTW!, 17.08.2026, 1/5: "The 2026 redesign is clearly meant to promote the paid subscription model and not for ergonomics. The post-session is now multiple screens showing among other unhelpful analytics the option to watch ads to boost reward. The new interface is buggy, slow, bloated, and often unresponsive. Useful features (e.g. adjusting focus time) are hidden behind unnecessary menus. The pay-to-unlock trees are background noise, but you can't ignore the constant nagging to watch ads or pay. The old interface was good precisely because it's minimalistic. I hope paid users can have the option to revert to the old interface. For other frustrated users: you can still use the interface if you turn on plant together. Apparently this feature will be removed once the new interface officially launches. I hope the developers have the decency to leave it in."
  2. w y, 09.07.2026, 1/5: "paid for forest plus and still got ads (not the optional ones for extra rewards)"
